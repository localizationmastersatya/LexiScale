About LexiScale

The human layer behind global products and better AI.

LexiScale helps brands adapt websites, apps, documents, learning content, games, and video for every market — and gives AI teams the multilingual speech data, annotation, and evaluation their models need. Every audience, and every model, gets language that feels clear, culturally tuned, and confidently on-brand. We combine vetted native linguists with a disciplined, three-layer quality system, and we operate under NDA from day one.

Our Mission

To make every audience — and every model — feel locally imagined, by pairing native human expertise with a rigorous, repeatable quality system.

Our Vision

To be the trusted human layer behind great localized products and better AI models worldwide, across 125+ languages.

AI Data Solutions

The human layer behind better AI models.

Great AI starts with great data, and great data starts with real people. Our vetted native-speaker network records, transcribes, annotates, and evaluates in 125+ languages — so the models you train sound less like machines and more like the people they serve.
